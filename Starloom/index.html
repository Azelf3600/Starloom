About the Studio

Small team.

Starloom is an independent game studio built on the belief that the most extraordinary experiences come from the most intentional craftsmanship. We weave together art, narrative, and mechanics into worlds that linger long after you put the controller down.

Every game we make carries a thread of wonder — something that surprises you, challenges you, or simply makes you feel something you didn't expect.
