About the Studio

Small team.
Big worlds.

Starloom is an independent game studio built on the belief that the most meaningful experiences are not just played but also understood. We craft worlds where creativity and intention meet, shaping every detail to leave a lasting impression beyond the screen

Our work is driven by a fascination with systems, patterns, and the hidden logic that governs how things move, respond, and evolve. Whether through action, strategy, or discovery, we design experiences that invite players to think, adapt, and uncover the rules beneath the surface.

Every game we make carries a thread of wonder, something that surprises you, challenges you, or simply makes you feel something you didn't expect.
