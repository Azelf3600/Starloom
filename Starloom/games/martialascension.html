About the Game

Where Strength Is Supreme

Martial Ascension is a cultivation fighting game set in the ancient world of Murim — a realm where warriors forge their Qi through grueling discipline, deadly combat, and unwavering will. In this world, the strong ascend. The weak are forgotten.

Choose your path. Master your martial arts. Battle rivals across three modes that test both your reflexes and your mastery of each fighter's unique cultivation style — from the ethereal sword techniques of the White Wind Dao to the suffocating poison arts of the Ten Thousand Sovereigns.

Battlegrounds

The Three Arenas

Stage I
Sword God Palace

The grand martial arena of the strongest sect in the righteous path. Every five years the younger generation gathers here to test their blades and claim glory beneath the eternal gaze of the Sword God's legacy.

Stage II
Black Forest

A decayed wilderness consumed by corrupted Qi. The trees are blackened and dead. No animals remain — only demonic beasts prowl the fog. A place where only the truly strong survive long enough to leave.

Stage III
Immortal Ancient Battlefield

The sacred ruin where the great war between immortals, demons, and beasts was fought and decided. Ancient Qi still bleeds from the earth. The echoes of those who fell here have never truly left.
